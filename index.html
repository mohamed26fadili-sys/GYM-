Training rules

  • First 2 weeks: consider 2 sets instead of 3, prioritize technique.
  • Most strength sets: stop with about 2 good reps left in reserve.
  • When you hit the top of the rep range for every set with control, increase load a little.
  • Rest 90–120 sec for compound lifts; 60–90 sec for accessories.
  • Warm up with 5–8 minutes easy movement plus 1–2 lighter ramp-up sets for your first big lift.

If you have pain, numbness, joint locking, dizziness, chest pain, faintness or unusual breathlessness, stop and seek appropriate medical advice. Consider a physio assessment for persistent posture-related symptoms.
